Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

RecipeUpgrader turns a dish name into a recipe that fits your fitness goal. This page explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with. RecipeUpgrader is an open-source project; you can read exactly how it handles data in its source code.

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile picture link, and Google account ID. We use these only to create your account and sign you in. We never see or store your Google password.
  • Recipes you save. If you save a recipe, we store the dish name, your goal (cutting, maintaining, or bulking), servings, and the generated recipes and nutrition numbers, linked to your account.
  • What you type into the app. The dish name, goal, and servings you enter are used to generate your recipe. They are not stored unless you save the recipe.
  • Location, only if you choose to share it. If you enter a ZIP code or click "Use my location", we use it to find nearby grocery stores. Coordinates are rounded to about 1 km before they are used. Your location is never saved to your account or written to our logs.
  • Basic technical logs. Our server records things like how long a request took, whether it succeeded, and which goal was chosen. These logs do not include your email, your location, or the dish names you enter. Your IP address is used briefly to prevent abuse (rate limiting) and is not stored with your account.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information or share it with advertisers.
  • We don't show ads or use tracking or advertising cookies.
  • We don't use your information to train AI models.

Your sign-in session is kept in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. It's removed when you sign out.

Services that process your information

To make the app work, some information is sent to these services:

  • Google - signing you in.
  • Supabase - managing your account and storing the recipes you save.
  • Nebius Token Factory (running NVIDIA Nemotron AI models) - receives the dish name, goal, servings, and recipe text to generate and rewrite your recipe. It does not receive your name or email.
  • USDA FoodData Central - receives ingredient names to look up nutrition data.
  • Zippopotam.us and OpenStreetMap - only if you share a location: receive your ZIP code or rounded coordinates to find nearby stores.
  • Tavily - only if you share a location: receives ingredient and store names to check what's sold nearby.
  • Cloudflare and Render - host the website and server. Like any web host, they process your IP address to deliver pages and requests.

Google user data

RecipeUpgrader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We only request your basic profile and email address, and use them only to sign you in.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete any saved recipe at any time from the Saved recipes page. Your account and saved recipes are kept until you ask us to delete them. To delete your account and everything linked to it, open an issue on our GitHub page asking for account deletion (don't include personal details there; we'll follow up). You can also remove RecipeUpgrader's access to your Google account at any time from your Google account settings.

Children

RecipeUpgrader is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will be noted on the site.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue on our GitHub page.
